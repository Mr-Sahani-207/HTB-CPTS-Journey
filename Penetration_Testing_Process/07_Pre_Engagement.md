# 7. Pre-Engagement Phase, Contracts & Rules of Engagement (RoE)

## 1. Three Main Steps of Pre-Engagement

1. **Scoping Questionnaire:**
   Sending a structured questionnaire to the client to determine the type of assessment required (External/Internal Pentest, Web Application, or Red Team), the number of target IPs/domains/hosts, and whether testing will be **Blackbox/Greybox/Whitebox** or **evasive** (stealthy).

2. **Pre-Engagement Meeting:**
   Reviewing the questionnaire responses with the client to align on exact scope boundaries, project timelines, third-party hosting permissions (e.g., cloud providers), potential operational risks, and commercial terms.

3. **Kick-Off Meeting:**
   Holding a final alignment call between the client's technical team and the penetration testers once all legal agreements are signed—finalizing the exact testing start window, communication channels, and emergency escalation procedures.

---

## 2. Three Types of Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)

* **Unilateral NDA:** Only one party is legally obligated to keep shared information confidential.
* **Bilateral NDA:** Both parties are obligated to protect each other's confidential information *(most common in penetration testing engagements)*.
* **Multilateral NDA:** Used when three or more parties are involved in the engagement.
