# 5. Cybersecurity Laws & Compliance Checklist

## 1. Key Cyber & Data Privacy Laws Across Major Jurisdictions

Different countries and regions enforce strict legal frameworks against unauthorized access, system tampering, and data exposure:

| Country / Region | Hacking & Cybercrime Laws | Data Privacy & Sector-Specific Laws |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **India** | **IT Act 2000** (criminalizes unauthorized access, data theft, and cyberattacks) | **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act** (governs personal data processing and privacy) |
| **USA** | **CFAA** (Computer Fraud and Abuse Act — prohibits unauthorized access to protected computer systems) | **HIPAA** (healthcare/medical data), **COPPA** (children under 13), **DMCA** (copyright & DRM bypass), **ECPA** (electronic communications interception) |
| **European Union (EU)** | **NIS2 Directive** & **Budapest Convention on Cybercrime** | **GDPR** (strict rules and heavy financial penalties for handling EU residents' personal data) |
| **United Kingdom (UK)** | **Computer Misuse Act 1990** (prohibits unauthorized access and system modification) | **Data Protection Act 2018**, **RIPA / IPA** (interception and surveillance regulations) |
| **China** | **Cybersecurity Law** & **National Security Law** | Strict security assessments and localization rules for cross-border data transfers |

*(Note: **GDPR** applies extraterritorially—any organization worldwide that processes the personal data of EU residents must comply with it, regardless of where the company is located.)*

## 2. Six Mandatory Precautions During a Penetration Test (Compliance Checklist)

To avoid legal liability and protect client operations, follow these six rules during every engagement:

1. **Obtain Written Consent:** Always secure a signed **Scope of Work (SoW)** and explicit written authorization from the system or network owner before starting any testing.
2. **Stay Strictly Within Scope:** Test only the IP addresses, domains, URLs, and testing windows explicitly defined in the signed agreement.
3. **Do No Harm:** Avoid running unstable exploits, denial-of-service payloads, or aggressive scans that could crash production servers or disrupt network availability.
4. **Respect Data Privacy:** Never access, copy, store, or disclose personal or sensitive client data beyond what is strictly necessary to prove a vulnerability.
5. **No Unauthorized Interception:** Never intercept electronic communications, emails, or live network traffic without explicit prior authorization.
6. **Handle Regulated Environments Carefully:** Never test healthcare systems (e.g., **HIPAA**-covered infrastructure), financial networks, or government systems without specialized clearance and explicit approval.
