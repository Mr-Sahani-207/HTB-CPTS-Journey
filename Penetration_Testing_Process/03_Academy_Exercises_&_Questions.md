# 3. Problem-Solving Mindset & Asking for Help Effectively

* **Why Questions Feel Open-Ended:**
  HTB intentionally leaves certain questions open-ended because in real-world engagements, clients simply ask you to *"find as many vulnerabilities as possible"* without providing step-by-step hints. However, every lab question is directly tied to the concepts taught within that specific module.

* **Struggling Is Part of the Process:**
  Getting stuck on a tough lab or making mistakes is where real learning happens—when you look back later, concepts that once felt difficult become second nature.

* **The Right Way to Ask for Help (Crucial Habit):**
  * Never ask blindly, *"Just give me the answer."*
  * Always document **what you have discovered so far** and **what you have already tried** first—writing this out often reveals the answer on its own, and peers on Discord or senior colleagues/leads will take your question seriously only when they see genuine effort.

* **Keep It Simple (Check the Basics First):**
  Whenever you have an intuition or hunch about an attack path, verify the simplest, most straightforward possibility first—the solution is often hiding right in front of you.
