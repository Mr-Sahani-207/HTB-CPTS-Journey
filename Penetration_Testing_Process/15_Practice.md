# 15. Skill-Building Blueprint (`2 → 3 → 5 → 1`) & Soft Skills

## 1. Four-Level Practice Blueprint (`2 → 3 → 5 → 1`)

Reading theory alone is not enough—the most effective hands-on progression on Hack The Box follows this structured cycle:

1. **2x Academy Modules:**
   Complete two Academy modules and solve their labs, then **re-solve them from scratch** while writing structured notes alongside both technical findings and non-technical (executive summary) documentation.

2. **3x Retired Machines (2 Easy + 1 Medium):**
   * Capture both user and root flags independently first and draft your own structured penetration test report.
   * Afterward, review **official/community write-ups** and **IppSec's YouTube walkthroughs** to identify alternative attack paths or missed enumeration steps, and update your notes accordingly.

3. **5x Active Machines (2 Easy + 2 Medium + 1 Hard):**
   * Because public write-ups are unavailable for active machines, they simulate a **true blackbox engagement**. Write a complete report for each machine and have both a technical peer and a non-technical reader proofread it for clarity.

4. **1x Pro Lab / Endgame:**
   * Practice on large, multi-host enterprise environments (including Active Directory networks) to connect individual techniques into a full end-to-end **Attack Chain**—from initial foothold to complete domain compromise.

---

## 2. Soft Skills & Final Study Advice

* **Communication Accounts for 50% of the Job:**
  Alongside technical exploitation skills, writing clear emails, leading client kick-off calls, and defending remediation recommendations during report walkthrough meetings are equally critical—practicing mock walkthroughs with peers helps build this confidence early.

* **Follow Modules in Sequential Order:**
  Complete the CPTS path modules strictly **in sequence**—skipping ahead creates foundational knowledge gaps that make later enterprise and Active Directory modules much harder.
